## RECOMMENDED STUDY ORDER

If you do not want to consume everything, use this sequence for Chapter 2:

1. **Odoo Beginner's Guide** (YouTube)
2. **Odoo Learn** (official slides platform)
3. **Official Odoo 19 documentation** (apps, modules, hosting)
4. **Browse `odoo/odoo` on GitHub** (look at `addons/` only)

That gives you **product overview + official reference + source-code exposure** before Chapter 3 functional apps.
